# Bay Area Tidal Marsh Wave Attenuation

This project explores how modeled wave attenuation varies across San Francisco Bay tidal marshes and how patterns relate spatially to community vulnerability.

## Research question

How does modeled wave attenuation vary across tidal marshes in the San Francisco Bay Area, and how do these patterns spatially relate to shoreline community vulnerability?
